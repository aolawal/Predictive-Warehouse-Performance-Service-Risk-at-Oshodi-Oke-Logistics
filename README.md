# 🚚 Predictive Warehouse Performance & Service Risk Analytics
## Transforming Warehouse Operations from Reactive Monitoring to Proactive Intervention

# Executive Summary

This project was designed to address a critical logistics challenge:

Warehouse managers often discover productivity losses, processing bottlenecks, backlog accumulation, and dispatch risks too late, resulting in missed service commitments, increased overtime costs, and customer dissatisfaction.

Rather than focusing solely on historical reporting, this solution combines Operational Performance Analytics with Predictive Risk Monitoring to identify service failures before they occur.

The platform enables warehouse and operations leaders to:

- Detect productivity deterioration early
- Forecast backlog growth
- Identify capacity constraints
- Flag orders at risk of missing dispatch deadlines
- Prioritize interventions before customer impact occurs

# Business Problem

The organization processes:

- 25,000 Orders
- 12,000 Shipments
- 3,000 Returns
- 1 Million Units Processed
- 150 Employees Across 5 Warehouses

Despite strong throughput, performance indicators reveal operational risks:

| KPI | Current Performance | Risk Assessment |
|---|---|---|
| On-Time Delivery | 90% | Below operational target |
| Return Rate | 12% | High |
| Late Shipments | 1,000 | Material service risk |
| Returns | 3,000 | Significant profitability leakage |

The challenge is not visibility.

The challenge is identifying operational risk early enough to prevent service failure.

# Executive Conclusion
Oshodi-Oke Logistics demonstrates strong operational scale, processing 25,000 orders, 12,000 shipments, and over 1 million units across its warehouse network. However, performance indicators such as 90% On-Time Delivery, 1,000 late shipments, and a 12% return rate reveal opportunities to improve operational reliability, service performance, and cost efficiency.

The analysis shows that the primary challenge is not order volume, warehouse capacity, or inventory levels. The real challenge is identifying operational risk early enough to prevent service failures.

To address this, the recommended strategic direction is to implement a Predictive Warehouse Performance & Service Risk Framework focused on:

- Identifying productivity losses and operational bottlenecks
- Predicting backlog accumulation before it impacts service levels
- Flagging customer orders at risk of missing dispatch deadlines
- Optimising labour and processing capacity
- Equipping managers with actionable early-warning intelligence
- By shifting from retrospective reporting to predictive operational management, the organisation can proactively intervene before delays occur, reduce avoidable costs, improve service reliability, and strengthen customer satisfaction.
